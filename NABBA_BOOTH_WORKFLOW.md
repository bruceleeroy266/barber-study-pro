## 2. Demo Scripts

### 2.1 30-Second Elevator Pitch

> "ASCYN PRO helps barber schools improve board pass rates through measurable, accessible, effective digital learning. Students don't just read the textbook — they learn, reinforce, test, and improve. And instructors see exactly who needs help before they fail. Want to see how it works?"

### 2.2 5-Minute Demo Script

**Opening (30 seconds)**
- "Thank you for stopping by. I'm going to show you something that shouldn't be revolutionary — but in barber education, it is."
- "Students do not simply read the textbook. They learn, reinforce, test, receive feedback, and improve. And every step is measured."

**Dashboard (45 seconds)**
- Point to Board Readiness Score: "78% board ready — weighted by what actually appears on the state board exam."
- Point to Focus Areas: "Specific gaps identified. Not vague suggestions. Specific chapters. Specific topics."
- "Instructors see the exact same thing for every student. No guessing. No surprises."

**Chapter 10 (45 seconds)**
- "Let me show you what studying actually looks like."
- "Chapter 10: Properties and Disorders of the Hair and Scalp — the most-tested chapter on state boards."
- Scroll through lesson: "Students don't just read. They interact with organized content — key terms, board exam alerts, practical application callouts."

**Flashcards (30 seconds)**
- Flip 2-3 cards: "After the lesson, students reinforce with active recall — the method that actually builds memory."
- "Notice the phonetic pronunciation. The memory aid. Every card is designed for how barber students actually study."

**Quiz (30 seconds)**
- "Then they test themselves. Not for a grade. For feedback."
- Show one question: "Every question is written with board exam language in mind."

**Wrong Answer (45 seconds)**
- Intentionally select wrong answer: "Let me show you the most important screen in the entire platform."
- Show explanation: "The platform doesn't just say 'Wrong.' It teaches. This is where learning actually happens."

**Progress (30 seconds)**
- "Every interaction feeds into a progress dashboard. Students see growth. Instructors see gaps."

**Close (15 seconds)**
- "Better retention. Better preparation. Measurable results."
- "Want to see the instructor view, or shall I tell you about our pilot program?"

### 2.3 10-Minute Demo Script

Follow 5-minute script, then extend:

**Instructor Dashboard (2 minutes)**
- "Instructors see the same data for every student in the class."
- Show at-risk students: "Identify who needs help before they fail."
- Open student detail: "Drill into individual readiness. See exactly which topics are weak."
- Show intervention notes: "Track follow-ups and interventions."

**Class Analytics (1 minute)**
- Show heatmap: "Class-wide trends inform teaching."
- Show report generation: "Export data for accreditation and reporting."

**Pilot Program (1 minute)**
- "Join our pilot program. Shape the product. Get early access."
- "Pilot schools get discounted pricing and direct input on features."

**Q&A (1 minute)**
- Answer questions
- Capture contact information

## 3. Role-Specific Flows

### 3.1 School Owner Flow

```
Greeting → "Are you a school owner?" → 
Hook: "Retention is revenue" → 
5-min demo → 
Focus: At-risk students, retention metrics → 
Close: Pilot program, bulk pricing → 
Capture contact
```

**Key Talking Points:**
- "See which students are at risk before they sit for the exam"
- "Competitive differentiator for enrollment marketing"
- "Bulk licensing available"
- "No IT required — students use their own devices"

### 3.2 Instructor Flow

```
Greeting → "Are you an instructor?" → 
Hook: "You can't quiz 30 students individually" → 
5-min demo → 
Focus: Time savings, targeted remediation → 
Close: Pilot program, classroom integration → 
Capture contact
```

**Key Talking Points:**
- "Automated progress tracking — no grading workload"
- "Know exactly who needs help with what topic"
- "Targeted remediation — not one-size-fits-all"
- "Flipped classroom potential"

### 3.3 NABBA Official Flow

```
Greeting → "Are you with NABBA or a state board?" → 
Hook: "Every quiz question is written with board exam language in mind" → 
10-min demo → 
Focus: Standardization, pass rates, public safety → 
Close: Partnership discussion, data sharing → 
Capture contact
```

**Key Talking Points:**
- "Standardized content across all users"
- "Progress tracking creates accountability"
- "Public safety focus — infection control, contraindications"
- "Data on pass rates and preparation effectiveness"

## 6. Troubleshooting Guide

### 6.1 Technical Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Internet down | Switch to local demo server or screenshots |
| Laptop crash | Switch to backup device |
| Projector failure | Use laptop screen, invite close viewing |
| Slow loading | Use offline mode or video backup |
| Audio issues | Demo without audio, use visual cues |

### 6.2 Common Questions

| Question | Response |
|----------|----------|
| "How much does it cost?" | "We have individual, school, and district pricing. Let me get you our pricing sheet." |
| "Is there an app?" | "Currently web-based, works on any device. Native app coming soon." |
| "Can I customize content?" | "Currently standardized for quality. Custom content is on our roadmap." |
| "How do you prevent cheating?" | "Randomized questions, no copy-paste, conceptual understanding focus." |
| "What about practical skills?" | "This covers theory. Practical is still shop-based." |
| "Is my data safe?" | "FERPA-aware practices, secure cloud storage, no PII selling." |

### 6.3 Difficult Situations

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Competitor bashing | "There are other solutions. We're focused on measurable outcomes." |
| Price objection | "Consider the cost of a student dropping out vs. the cost of our platform." |
| Feature request | "Great idea. Let me note that for our product team." |
| Technical deep-dive | "I'd love to connect you with our technical team after the conference." |
| Time waster | "I don't want to keep you. Here's a one-pager — reach out if you'd like to learn more." |
