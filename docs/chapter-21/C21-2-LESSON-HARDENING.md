# C21-2 — Chapter 21 Lesson Hardening

## Parent architecture
C21-2 is stacked on C21-1 head `7feae8b64ebc25c5570912610e47b7647f675fcc`.

The approved eight-family Chapter 21 architecture remains unchanged.

## Scope
C21-2 hardens the learner lesson and binds it directly to the canonical architecture.

This phase does **not** change:

- the 60-card flashcard inventory;
- the 17-question chapter assessment;
- the 80% chapter-assessment threshold;
- the shared 20/10/40/15/15 grading weights;
- remediation or reassessment runtime behavior;
- instructor/school-admin diagnostics.

## Canonical lesson binding
Each real LO section now uses the canonical LO ID as its runtime `standardId` and is tagged in rendered lesson markup with both the canonical LO and concept family.

The former source IDs `CH21-LO01` through `CH21-LO08` are retained in learner markup as `data-legacy-standard-id` values for traceability.

## Shop-opening hardening
The lesson no longer presents three-to-six months of cash as a universal requirement.

It now teaches students to estimate an operating reserve from:

- expected fixed costs;
- expected revenue ramp;
- financing terms;
- risk tolerance;
- actual business conditions.

Business registration, tax IDs, licensing, zoning, occupancy, health-related permits, and other approvals are explicitly treated as jurisdiction- and structure-dependent requirements that should be verified from current official sources.

## Ownership and entity hardening
C21-2 removes categorical claims that:

- one structure is automatically best;
- an LLC creates absolute personal-asset protection;
- corporations can be summarized as "double taxation unless S corporation";
- the most common structure is necessarily the correct structure.

The lesson now compares:

- liability exposure;
- ownership;
- governance;
- tax treatment;
- filings;
- insurance;
- financing;
- future plans.

It directs students to current legal and tax guidance when choosing an entity.

## Recordkeeping hardening
Record retention is no longer presented as one fixed period.

The lesson now explains that retention depends on:

- document type;
- tax issue;
- jurisdiction;
- business structure;
- employment duties;
- other legal or regulatory requirements.

Client-record guidance now includes minimum-necessary collection, privacy/security, and access protection.

## Booth-rental / worker-status hardening
The lesson now explicitly states that the label "booth renter" does not determine worker classification, tax treatment, or business obligations.

Students are directed to evaluate:

- the actual written agreement;
- control of schedule/pricing/methods;
- payment handling;
- expenses;
- client records;
- insurance;
- licensing;
- tools/supplies;
- real facts and circumstances.

The fixed 25–30% tax-reserve rule and categorical quarterly-tax language were removed.

## Operations and safety boundary
Cleanliness remains an important shop-operations concept, but Chapter 21 explicitly defers infection-control and immediate bodily-safety procedures to the dedicated safety curriculum and current board/legal requirements.

C21-2 does not invent a Chapter 21 bodily-safety family.

## Advertising, privacy, and consent hardening
The lesson now teaches that:

- advertising does not guarantee a full appointment book;
- campaigns should be measured and adjusted;
- platform/channel usefulness depends on audience, cost, rules, and consent;
- reviews and testimonials must be truthful;
- a social-media tag is not automatically advertising consent;
- identifiable client images require clear permission;
- promotions should state important terms clearly;
- applicable privacy, advertising, communications, platform, school, and shop rules should be followed.

## Application anchors
C21-2 adds eight learner-facing, ungraded **Apply It** anchors:

1. `ch21-apply-01` — choosing an entry path;
2. `ch21-apply-02` — opening-shop due diligence;
3. `ch21-apply-03` — comparing ownership structures;
4. `ch21-apply-04` — testing a business plan;
5. `ch21-apply-05` — recordkeeping and retention verification;
6. `ch21-apply-06` — analyzing a booth-rental agreement;
7. `ch21-apply-07` — diagnosing shop operations;
8. `ch21-apply-08` — planning a compliant promotion.

These are explicitly ungraded in C21-2. They do not create durable evidence or modify mastery.

## Future micro-check placement
The C21-1 plan remains unchanged:

- one micro-check block after each LO;
- exactly two fresh questions per block;
- future C21-5 total: **16 micro-check questions**.

## Criticality boundary
Chapter 21 still has **zero bodily-safety families**.

The five compliance/legal-critical families remain:

- Shop Opening & Planning
- Ownership & Legal Structures
- Recordkeeping & Financial Compliance
- Booth Rental & Independent Business Responsibilities
- Advertising, Marketing & Client Consent

These must not inherit the 100% urgent-safety recovery rule merely because the underlying legal or financial consequence is serious.
