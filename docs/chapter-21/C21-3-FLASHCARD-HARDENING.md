## Business-entry and opening repairs
Cards 001–010 were hardened to avoid:

- calling one business path universally lower risk;
- assuming rental terms are always flat;
- stating that one side always controls schedule, pricing, or client records;
- treating one startup-reserve duration as a rule;
- treating worker structure labels as enough to determine legal status.

The deck now requires comparison of the actual agreement, working relationship, startup assumptions, financing, client demand, fixed costs, and applicable rules.

## Ownership/legal-structure repairs
Cards 011–020 now avoid:

- absolute personal-liability statements;
- absolute LLC asset-protection claims;
- reducing corporate taxation to one simplistic double-tax rule;
- implying one entity is automatically best;
- treating franchise terms as universal.

The hardened cards teach that liability, tax treatment, governance, filing, insurance, and franchise obligations depend on the actual entity, elections, agreements, conduct, and current law.

## Financial-plan repairs
Business-plan cards retain the original curriculum but now require financial forecasts to use documented assumptions and realistic ranges rather than treating projections as guarantees.

## Recordkeeping and tax repairs
Cards 031–040 now:

- frame financial records as examples rather than one exhaustive universal list;
- apply minimum-necessary/privacy principles to client information;
- explain that cash is not automatically exempt from tax/reporting;
- determine tax responsibility from actual classification and structure rather than the booth-renter label;
- remove the fixed **25–30%** tax-reserve rule;
- require verification of current tax obligations;
- require the rental agreement to identify expenses and operating responsibilities.

## Operations repairs
The cleanliness card now treats cleanliness as an operations responsibility while explicitly deferring infection-control and sanitation procedures to the dedicated safety curriculum and current requirements.

Retail/add-on and slow-day promotion cards were narrowed to avoid promising revenue outcomes or assuming one tactic always works.

## Advertising / consent repairs
Cards 051–060 now:

- use general advertising-channel concepts rather than depending on one named platform;
- reject the idea that frequency always beats quality;
- require genuine, non-misleading reviews/testimonials;
- require appropriate disclosure when incentives or promotional terms matter;
- require clear permission before using identifiable client images;
- recognize privacy, advertising, platform, school, and shop rules;
- frame local cross-promotion as an ethical, mutually understood arrangement;
- treat rebooking as a retention tool rather than a guaranteed full-book strategy.
