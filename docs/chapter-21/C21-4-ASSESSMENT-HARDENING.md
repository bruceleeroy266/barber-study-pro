## Key assessment repairs

### Business entry / rental
Q02 no longer treats booth rental as automatic proof of independent-contractor status or guaranteed control over pricing/scheduling.

It now requires review of the actual agreement, working relationship, expenses, control, and responsibilities.

### Entity / liability
Q04 no longer teaches one absolute statement about unlimited liability without qualification.

Q05 no longer makes "LLC" the universally recommended first-time-owner answer.

The hardened assessment requires comparison of liability, tax, governance, filing, insurance, and financing considerations and states that entity protection is not absolute.

### Booth-rental responsibilities / taxes
Q06, Q09, and Q11 no longer assume that every booth renter:

- is automatically independent;
- handles taxes in the same way;
- pays self-employment tax;
- supplies the same items;
- controls the same business functions.

These questions now test the actual agreement, worker classification, control, payment handling, insurance, tools, supplies, tax treatment, licensing, scheduling/pricing rights, and client records.

### Shop operations
Q13 now requires review of demand, staffing, pricing, rebooking, and campaign data before choosing a targeted response to slow periods.

It no longer treats one promotion tactic as the universal best answer.

### Advertising
Q14 no longer implies advertising guarantees a full book.

Q15 no longer teaches one-platform consistency as a universal formula.

Q16 now requires authentic, non-misleading reviews/referrals and recognition of platform, advertising, and incentive-disclosure rules.

### Startup reserve
Q17 removes the fixed **3–6 month** reserve answer.

The hardened question requires a reserve based on projected costs, financing, expected revenue ramp, and risk, and explicitly states that no universal number of months guarantees success.

## Criticality boundary
Chapter 21 continues to have:

- **0 bodily-safety families**
- **5 compliance/legal-critical families**

No legal, tax, licensing, entity, privacy, advertising, or financial error is promoted into the 100% urgent-safety recovery path merely because it is serious.
