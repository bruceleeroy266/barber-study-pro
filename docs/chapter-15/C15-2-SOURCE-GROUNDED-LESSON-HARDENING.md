# C15-2 — Source-Grounded Lesson Hardening

C15-2 reviews the existing Chapter 15 lesson while preserving the 54-section architecture certified in C15-1.

## Source boundary

The repository contains the original Chapter 15 textbook-image set plus source-derived planning and analysis reports created from those images. Those materials support the chapter's core subject matter: men's hair replacement consultation, alternative options, hair/base materials, stock and custom systems, attachment, maintenance, chemical-service considerations, and cutting/blending.

The prior lesson also included claims that were broader than the available source support or that should not be presented as universal rules. C15-2 therefore preserves the educational structure while narrowing unsupported medical, regulatory, manufacturer-dependent, and board-exam-certainty language.

No textbook prose was copied into the lesson.

## Repairs made

- removed the exact "second fastest-growing" market ranking because the repository's own source-derived reports conflict on the comparison category
- removed hard-coded Minoxidil effectiveness timing and sex/formulation claims from the lesson
- removed comparative Finasteride effectiveness language and a narrow side-effect list
- replaced physician-only prescribing language with referral to an appropriate licensed healthcare professional
- changed universal barber scope claims into jurisdiction-aware scope guidance
- removed the unsupported three-year model-release retention rule
- replaced absolute lace/tape damage rules with manufacturer/system-specific attachment guidance
- replaced a universal 24–48 hour bonding rule with adhesive-manufacturer cure guidance while acknowledging that some source material presents a 24–48 hour window
- repaired the internal contradiction that described permanent waving with a floating technique while also saying cold-waving is never performed on systems
- made chemical-service guidance depend on fiber, base, prior processing, and manufacturer approval
- removed broad claims that specific points are guaranteed or frequent state-board questions
- renamed the Board Exam Alert/Checkpoint lesson framing into professional/knowledge-checkpoint framing
