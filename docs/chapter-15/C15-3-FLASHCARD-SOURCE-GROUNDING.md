# C15-3 — Flashcard Source-Grounding & Concept Certification

C15-3 independently audits the complete 90-card Chapter 15 flashcard bank against the C15-2 hardened lesson and the repository's Chapter 15 Milady-derived planning/analysis source set.

## Locked architecture

- 90 / 90 flashcard IDs preserved
- all C15-1 flashcard-to-concept mappings preserved
- all seven Chapter 15 concept families retain flashcard coverage
- shared activity-evidence resolution remains intact
- 54 lesson sections remain unchanged in C15-3
- 72 assessment questions remain unchanged in C15-3

## Audit approach

Every card was reviewed for consistency with the hardened C15-2 lesson and for inherited claims involving:

- medical treatment, medication strengths, dosing, efficacy, adverse effects, and referral
- barbering scope and jurisdiction-specific legal boundaries
- medical/surgical procedures and expected outcomes
- client-image permission and record retention
- material and base superlatives
- lace attachment and manufacturer-dependent procedures
- bonding water resistance and cure time
- cleaning temperature, storage, rotation, and maintenance schedules
- permanent waving, lightening, coloring, and chemical-service limits
- stock/custom fit claims
- board-exam certainty and frequency claims

## Repairs

The affected flashcards were hardened in place without changing IDs. Repairs include:

- removing the unsupported market-growth ranking
- removing exam-certainty language from terminology and "trap" cards
- replacing Minoxidil strength/efficacy and Finasteride side-effect directives with referral-based guidance
- making scope claims jurisdiction-aware
- removing lifetime transplant-outcome language
- removing the unsupported three-year photo-release retention rule
- narrowing human-hair, synthetic-fiber, wool/allergy, base-material, knotting, and custom-fit absolutes
- making lace attachment, bonding, water exposure, spirit gum, cleaning, storage, and cure time manufacturer-dependent
- preserving the source-derived one-week / three-to-four-week cleaning interval only as a study baseline
- removing the universal two-system requirement
- making chemical services depend on fiber, base, prior processing, and manufacturer approval
- converting "Board Exam Trap" cards into neutral "Knowledge Trap" cards

## Source boundary

The repository contains 15 Chapter 15 textbook screenshots and source-derived Chapter 15 planning/analysis documents. C15-3 uses those materials together with the C15-2 hardened lesson as the working source boundary. Where the source-derived material contains an overbroad medical, regulatory, manufacturer-dependent, or exam-frequency statement, the flashcards follow the narrower C15-2 formulation rather than repeating the unsupported absolute.

No textbook prose was copied.
