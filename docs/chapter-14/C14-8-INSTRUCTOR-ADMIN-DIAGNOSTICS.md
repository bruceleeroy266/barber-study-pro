# C14-8 — Instructor/School-Admin Diagnostics + Live Five-Component Grade\n\nC14-8 exposes the Chapter 14 learning-evidence chain through the same authorized student oversight route used by instructors and school administrators.\n\n## Diagnostic model\n\nChapter 14 staff diagnostics now show the live academic grade, overall concept mastery/confidence, micro-check percentage, chapter-assessment percentage, latest five-question reassessment, active remediation target, strongest/weakest concepts, per-concept observations, preserved initial misses, reassessment-correct counts, recent evidence time, and active safety intervention state. Internal concept IDs are not displayed in the staff UI.\n\n## Live grade\n\nThe route uses the shared unchanged five-component weighting: micro-checks 20%, flashcards 10%, chapter assessment 40%, scenario/application 15%, and remediation/reassessment 15%. Flashcard/scenario percentages come from durable chapter_activity_evidence rows; micro-check evidence comes from immutable chapter_micro_check_attempts; assessment/reassessment comes from quiz attempts. A grade stays provisional while required evidence is incomplete.\n\n## Completion and recovery\n\nCompletion remains separate in student_progress. Original incorrect first attempts remain visible after reassessment; successful recovery adds reassessment evidence and can improve mastery without erasing historical misses.\n\n## Safety visibility\n\nC14-6 safety state flows into staff diagnostics. Urgent state shows instructor review, formal five-question safety reassessment, and 100% requirement.\n\n## Shared instructor/school-admin route\n\nNo duplicate school-admin calculation path was created. The school-owner Student Performance panel links to /instructor/student/[studentId], which already authorizes instructor/admin/school-admin roles with same-school evidence restrictions. C14-8 extends that same evidence read through ch-14.\n\n## Certification gate\n\nC14-8 is GREEN only after Chapter 14 diagnostics preserve misses/recovery, expose safety state, use live 20/10/40/15/15 evidence, keep completion separate, reuse the same authorized route, extend cross-chapter oversight tests through Chapter 14, and both exact-head Engineering Verification and Vercel are GREEN.\n
