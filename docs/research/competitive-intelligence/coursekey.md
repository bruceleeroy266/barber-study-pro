## ASCYN PRO Implication

**Differentiation Assessment:**

CourseKey/Cortex is particularly strong in:
- Student retention risk
- Academic risk
- Attendance risk
- Lateness
- Inactivity
- Engagement
- Daily risk scoring
- Student prioritization
- Automated outreach
- Intervention alerts

**Therefore:** ASCYN PRO should NOT attempt to differentiate simply through:
> Detect → Alert → Intervene

CourseKey already provides sophisticated intervention-oriented retention workflows.

---

## Strategic Response

ASCYN PRO's potential differentiation must be more specific than generic intervention. The focus should be on **licensure-specific learning gaps** and **targeted remediation tied to exam content**:

```
Exam-Aligned Learning Gap
    ↓
Instructor Action (with exam context)
    ↓
Targeted Remediation (specific to exam domains)
    ↓
Follow-Up (retesting on same domains)
    ↓
Evidence of Improvement (exam-readiness progression)
```

The distinction is **licensure-specific remediation**, not generic student success intervention.
