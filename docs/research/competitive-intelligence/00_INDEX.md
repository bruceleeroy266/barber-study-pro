## Key Findings Summary

### Pivot Point

**Confirmed Overlap:**
- Student performance tracking
- Assessment percentages
- Weak-area identification
- Exam-category performance
- Adaptive quizzes
- Mock exams
- Instructor analytics
- At-risk identification
- Progress monitoring

**ASCYN PRO Implication:** These capabilities alone are NOT sufficient differentiation.

### Milady

**Confirmed Overlap:**
- Exam preparation
- Diagnostic assessments
- Topic mastery
- Personalized study
- CIB alignment
- Exam weighting
- Instructor insight
- State-specific preparation

**ASCYN PRO Implication:** Exam-blueprint alignment alone is NOT sufficient differentiation.

### CourseKey

**Confirmed Strengths:**
- Student retention risk
- Academic risk
- Attendance risk
- Lateness tracking
- Inactivity monitoring
- Engagement metrics
- Daily risk scoring
- Student prioritization
- Automated outreach
- Intervention alerts

**ASCYN PRO Implication:** Generic "Detect → Alert → Intervene" alone is NOT sufficient differentiation.

## Competitive Guardrail

**Absence of public evidence does NOT mean a competitor lacks a capability.**

Use: **"Not confirmed in reviewed public sources"**

Not: **"Competitor does not have this"**

unless authoritative evidence supports that conclusion.
