## Critical Finding: CIB Alignment

**Milady ExamReady already aligns exam preparation to Candidate Information Bulletins**, including:
- Exam topics
- Respective weights
- NIC examinations
- PSI examinations
- Select state examinations

Milady also provides instructors with **topic-level insight into student learning gaps**.

---

## ASCYN PRO Implication

**Differentiation Assessment:**

Milady already provides significant functionality around:
- Curriculum
- Exam preparation
- Diagnostic assessments
- Topic mastery
- Personalized study
- CIB alignment
- Exam weighting
- Instructor insight
- State-specific preparation

**Therefore:** ASCYN PRO should NOT position itself as simply:
> "Exam prep aligned to the licensing examination."

That market already has established competitors.

**Exam/CIB alignment may still become an important part of ASCYN PRO's architecture, but it should be considered an enabling capability rather than the entire value proposition.**

---

## Strategic Response

ASCYN PRO's potential differentiation must extend beyond CIB alignment to the **instructor-centered workflow**:

```
Learning Gap → Instructor Action → Targeted Remediation → Follow-Up → Evidence of Improvement
```

The emphasis is on **what happens after a gap is identified**, not the alignment itself.
