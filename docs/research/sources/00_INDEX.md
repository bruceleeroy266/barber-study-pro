## Source Reliability Standard

### Highest Reliability

- Official state licensing board websites
- Official exam developer websites (NIC, PSI)
- Official exam administrator websites (Prov, Prometric, DL Roope)
- Official Candidate Information Bulletins (CIBs)

### High Reliability

- Official competitor websites
- Official competitor product documentation
- Press releases from official organizations

### Medium Reliability

- Industry trade publications
- Conference presentations
- Webinars from official organizations

### Low Reliability — Verify

- Third-party 50-state summaries
- Blog posts
- Forum discussions
- Social media

**Rule:** Low-reliability sources may be used for discovery but must be verified against highest-reliability sources before becoming authoritative ASCYN PRO data.

```
Source identified

    ↓

```
