## Primary Hypothesis

### H1: Instructor-Centered Workflow Value

**Hypothesis:** Schools will value an instructor-centered workflow that connects learning gaps to targeted remediation with documented evidence of improvement.

**Validation Method:** Structured interviews

**Success Criteria:**
- 70%+ of schools confirm this addresses a real pain point
- Schools can articulate specific scenarios where this would help

**Evidence to Collect:**
- Quotes about current pain points
- Descriptions of current workarounds
- Reactions to Isabella demo

---

## Secondary Hypotheses

### H2: Complement vs. Replace

**Hypothesis:** Schools will adopt ASCYN PRO as a complement to existing systems rather than requiring replacement.

**Validation Method:** Structured interviews

**Success Criteria:**
- 50%+ express interest in complementary adoption
- Schools can describe how ASCYN PRO would fit with existing tools

**Evidence to Collect:**
- Current system inventory
- Integration requirements
- Objections to replacement

---

### H3: Licensure-Specific Differentiation

**Hypothesis:** Focusing specifically on licensure exam preparation provides meaningful differentiation from generic student success platforms.

**Validation Method:** Structured interviews

**Success Criteria:**
- Schools articulate distinction between retention and exam-readiness
- Schools confirm licensure-specific focus is valuable

**Evidence to Collect:**
- Comparison to CourseKey-style platforms
- Specific licensure pain points
- Exam-readiness vs. retention priorities

---

### H4: Evidence of Improvement Requirement

**Hypothesis:** Schools need documented evidence that remediation worked, not just that it occurred.

**Validation Method:** Structured interviews

**Success Criteria:**
- Schools confirm documentation of outcomes is a pain point
- Schools describe accreditation or accountability requirements

**Evidence to Collect:**
- Current documentation practices
- Accreditation requirements
- Audit experiences

---

### H5: Willingness to Pay

**Hypothesis:** Schools will pay $999/year for ASCYN PRO's school license.

**Validation Method:** Structured interviews + follow-up

**Success Criteria:**
- 30%+ express willingness to pay at proposed price point
- Schools can articulate ROI justification

**Evidence to Collect:**
- Price sensitivity feedback
- Budget allocation for educational technology
- Comparison to current spending

---

### H6: Multi-System Fatigue

**Hypothesis:** Instructors are frustrated by using multiple systems and would value tighter integration.

**Validation Method:** Structured interviews

**Success Criteria:**
- Schools confirm multi-system friction
- Instructors describe workflow inefficiencies

**Evidence to Collect:**
- System inventory
- Workflow descriptions
- Integration wishlist

## Post-Validation Decision Framework

### Strong Validation (70%+)

**Decision:** Proceed with architecture planning

**Actions:**
1. Prioritize validated requirements
2. Develop post-NABBA roadmap
3. Begin architecture design
4. Plan pilot expansion

---

### Partial Validation (40-69%)

**Decision:** Refine hypothesis; identify specific pain points

**Actions:**
1. Analyze which aspects resonated
2. Refine positioning
3. Adjust feature priorities
4. Conduct follow-up interviews

---

### Weak Validation (<40%)

**Decision:** Pivot strategy; explore alternative positioning

**Actions:**
1. Analyze why hypothesis failed
2. Identify alternative pain points
3. Explore different positioning
4. Consider product pivot
