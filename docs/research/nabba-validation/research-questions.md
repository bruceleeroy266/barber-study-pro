# NABBA Research Questions

**Document:** Research Questions for School Interviews  
**Event:** NABBA Conference  
**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Status:** ACTIVE

---

## Interview Structure

**Duration:** 5-10 minutes per school  
**Format:** Structured interview with open-ended follow-ups  
**Capture:** Written notes + optional audio recording (with permission)

---

## Questions for School Owners

### Current State

1. **How do you currently identify students who are not prepared for their licensing theory exam?**
   - Probe: What systems or processes do you use?
   - Probe: How early can you identify at-risk students?

2. **What happens after you identify a learning gap?**
   - Probe: Who is responsible for intervening?
   - Probe: What resources do you use for remediation?

3. **How is intervention documented?**
   - Probe: Is there a formal process or is it informal?
   - Probe: How do you track whether intervention occurred?

4. **How do you determine whether remediation worked?**
   - Probe: Do you retest students after remediation?
   - Probe: How do you document improvement?

### System Usage

5. **Do instructors have to use multiple systems to manage student progress?**
   - Probe: Which systems do you use?
   - Probe: What friction does this create?

6. **What does your existing system fail to show you?**
   - Probe: What information would be most valuable?
   - Probe: What reports or analytics are missing?

### Value Proposition

7. **Would tighter connection between licensing-exam content and instructor intervention be useful?**
   - Probe: Why or why not?
   - Probe: What would that look like in practice?

8. **What would make a system like ASCYN PRO worth paying for?**
   - Probe: What features or capabilities are most valuable?
   - Probe: What price point seems reasonable?

---

## Questions for Instructors

### Current State

1. **How do you know which students need help before the licensing exam?**
   - Probe: What signals do you look for?
   - Probe: How do you prioritize which students to help?

2. **What do you do when you identify a struggling student?**
   - Probe: What resources do you use?
   - Probe: How do you track what you've tried?

3. **How do you track whether your intervention worked?**
   - Probe: Do you retest students?
   - Probe: How do you know if they're ready for the exam?

### Pain Points

4. **What frustrates you most about preparing students for licensure?**
   - Probe: What takes the most time?
   - Probe: What information do you wish you had?

5. **Do you have to use multiple systems to manage student progress?**
   - Probe: How does this affect your workflow?
   - Probe: What would make your job easier?

## Demo-Specific Questions

### After Isabella Demo

1. **Does this workflow reflect how you would want to intervene with students?**
   - Probe: What's missing?
   - Probe: What would you change?

2. **Would this complement your existing systems or require replacement?**
   - Probe: How would this fit with your current tools?
   - Probe: What integration would you need?

3. **What would make you recommend this to your school owner?**
   - Probe: What benefits are most compelling?
   - Probe: What concerns would you have?

## Closing Questions

1. **Can we follow up with you after the conference?**
   - Capture: Name, school, email, phone

2. **Would you be interested in a pilot program?**
   - Capture: Interest level, timeline

3. **Is there anyone else at your school we should talk to?**
   - Capture: Referral contacts
