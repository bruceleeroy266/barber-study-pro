## Feedback Collection Plan

### Target Participants

| Role | Goal | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| School owners | Understand purchasing decisions | Primary decision-makers |
| Instructors | Understand daily workflow needs | Primary users |
| School administrators | Understand administrative needs | Secondary users |

### Feedback Categories

| Category | Questions to Explore |
|----------|---------------------|
| Current tools | What systems do you currently use? |
| Pain points | What's missing from your current tools? |
| Remediation workflow | How do you currently handle students who are struggling? |
| Instructor analytics | What data do you wish you had about your students? |
| Exam preparation | How do you prepare students for the licensing exam? |
| Purchasing | What would make you consider a new tool? |
| Pricing | What would you expect to pay? |
| Integration | Would you prefer a standalone tool or one that works with your existing systems? |
