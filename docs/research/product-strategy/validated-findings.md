## Competitive Research Findings

### Finding: Milady ExamReady CIB Alignment

**Finding:** Milady ExamReady already aligns exam preparation to Candidate Information Bulletins, including exam topics and their respective weights.

**Source:** Milady product documentation  
**Verified:** 2026-08-14  
**Confidence:** CONFIRMED

**ASCYN PRO Implication:** Exam/CIB alignment alone cannot be treated as a competitive differentiator.

---

### Finding: Pivot Point Feature Overlap

**Finding:** Pivot Point provides significant functionality around student performance, assessment percentages, weak-area identification, exam-category performance, adaptive quizzes, mock exams, instructor analytics, at-risk identification, and progress monitoring.

**Source:** Pivot Point product documentation  
**Verified:** 2026-08-14  
**Confidence:** CONFIRMED

**ASCYN PRO Implication:** These capabilities alone are not sufficient differentiation.

### Finding: CourseKey Intervention Strength

## Licensing Research Findings

### Finding: Oklahoma Exam Structure

**Finding:** Oklahoma barber theory uses NIC examination content administered through Prov.

**Source:** Oklahoma State Board, NIC, Prov  
**Verified:** 2026-08-14  
**Confidence:** CONFIRMED

**ASCYN PRO Implication:** Must distinguish exam developer from exam administrator in architecture.

---

### Finding: National Administration Fragmentation

**Finding:** Barber/cosmetology examination administration involves multiple organizations including Prov, PSI, Prometric, DL Roope, PCS, and individual state boards/agencies.

**Source:** Initial national research  
**Verified:** 2026-08-14  
**Confidence:** CONFIRMED

**ASCYN PRO Implication:** Future architecture should NOT assume a single national exam provider.

---

### Finding: PSI Role Complexity

**Finding:** PSI can play more than one role depending on the jurisdiction: administrator, infrastructure provider, and developer of PSI-developed national examination content.

**Source:** PSI documentation, Texas research  
**Verified:** 2026-08-14  
**Confidence:** CONFIRMED

**ASCYN PRO Implication:** `PSI` should not automatically mean one specific examination blueprint.

---

### Finding: CIB Versioning

**Finding:** Candidate Information Bulletins and exam blueprints can change. Current NIC Barber materials have explicit effective/revision dates.

**Source:** NIC CIB documentation  
**Verified:** 2026-08-14  
**Confidence:** CONFIRMED

**ASCYN PRO Implication:** Future architecture must distinguish Exam → Exam Version → Effective Date → CIB → Blueprint.
