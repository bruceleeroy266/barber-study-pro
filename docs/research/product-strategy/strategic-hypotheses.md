## Primary Strategic Hypothesis

### Hypothesis: Instructor-Centered Remediation Workflow

**Status:** STRATEGIC HYPOTHESIS — NOT YET FULLY VALIDATED

**Statement:**

ASCYN PRO can differentiate by focusing on the instructor-centered workflow after a learning gap is identified:

```
Learning Gap
    ↓
Instructor Action
    ↓
Targeted Remediation
    ↓
Follow-Up
    ↓
Evidence of Improvement
```

**Context:** within licensure preparation

---

## Core Questions

1. **Who needs my attention?**
2. **What exactly are they struggling with?**
3. **What should happen next?**
4. **What intervention occurred?**
5. **Did the student improve afterward?**

---

## Supporting Hypotheses

### H1: Complement Existing Systems

**Statement:** ASCYN PRO can operate as a specialized layer alongside existing curriculum/education systems rather than requiring replacement.

**Status:** STRATEGIC HYPOTHESIS / VALIDATION REQUIRED

**Test at NABBA:** Ask schools if they would adopt a system that complements their existing Pivot Point/Milady/CourseKey infrastructure.

---

### H2: Licensure-Specific Focus

**Statement:** Focusing specifically on licensure exam preparation (vs. generic student success) provides meaningful differentiation.

**Status:** STRATEGIC HYPOTHESIS / VALIDATION REQUIRED

**Rationale:** CourseKey focuses on retention/attendance; ASCYN PRO would focus on exam-domain mastery.

---

### H3: Workflow Integration

**Statement:** The integration of gap identification → remediation → verification in a single workflow is valuable to instructors.

**Status:** STRATEGIC HYPOTHESIS / VALIDATION REQUIRED

**Test at NABBA:** Demonstrate the Isabella demo flow and gauge instructor reaction.

---

### H4: Evidence of Improvement

**Statement:** Schools need documented evidence that remediation worked, not just that it occurred.

**Status:** STRATEGIC HYPOTHESIS / VALIDATION REQUIRED

**Rationale:** Accreditation and accountability require outcome documentation.

---

## Anti-Hypotheses (What We Should NOT Claim)

### AH1: Exam Blueprint Alignment is Unique

**Statement:** ASCYN PRO's exam blueprint alignment is a unique differentiator.

**Status:** REJECTED

**Evidence:** Milady ExamReady already provides CIB-aligned preparation.

---

### AH2: Generic Analytics are Differentiating

**Statement:** Student performance percentages, weak-area identification, and adaptive quizzes differentiate ASCYN PRO.

**Status:** REJECTED

**Evidence:** Pivot Point already provides these capabilities.

---

### AH3: Generic Intervention is Differentiating

**Statement:** Detect → Alert → Intervene workflows differentiate ASCYN PRO.

**Status:** REJECTED

**Evidence:** CourseKey already provides sophisticated intervention workflows.

---

### AH4: Pass Probability Prediction

**Statement:** ASCYN PRO can predict licensing exam pass probability.

**Status:** REJECTED

**Evidence:** No real-world outcome dataset to support predictive validity.

## Validation Plan

### NABBA Conference (2026-09-20)

**Method:** Structured interviews with school owners and instructors

**Questions:**
1. How do you currently identify students who are not prepared for their licensing theory exam?
2. What happens after you identify a learning gap?
3. Who is responsible for intervening?
4. How is intervention documented?
5. How do you determine whether remediation worked?
6. Do instructors have to use multiple systems?
7. What does your existing system fail to show you?
8. Would tighter connection between licensing-exam content and instructor intervention be useful?
9. What would make a system like ASCYN PRO worth paying for?

**Success Criteria:**
- 70%+ of schools confirm the workflow addresses a real pain point
- 50%+ express interest in a complementary system
- 30%+ request follow-up information
