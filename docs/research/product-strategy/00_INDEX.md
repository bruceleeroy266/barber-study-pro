## Current Strategic Hypothesis

**Status:** STRATEGIC HYPOTHESIS — NOT YET FULLY VALIDATED

ASCYN PRO is investigating an instructor-centered workflow around:

```
Learning Gap
    ↓
Instructor Action
    ↓
Targeted Remediation
    ↓
Follow-Up
    ↓
Evidence of Improvement
```

within the context of licensure preparation.

**Do NOT document this as proven competitive exclusivity.** NABBA will help validate the hypothesis.

## NABBA Validation Focus

NABBA is product validation. Key questions to answer:

1. How do schools currently identify students not prepared for licensing theory exams?
2. What happens after a learning gap is identified?
3. Who is responsible for intervening?
4. How is intervention documented?
5. How do schools determine whether remediation worked?
6. Do instructors have to use multiple systems?
7. What does the existing system fail to show?
8. Would tighter connection between licensing-exam content and instructor intervention be useful?
9. What would make a system like ASCYN PRO worth paying for?
