## Hypotheses Requiring Validation

### H1: Instructor-Centered Workflow Value

**Hypothesis:** Schools will value an instructor-centered workflow that connects learning gaps to targeted remediation with documented evidence of improvement.

**Current Status:** UNVALIDATED

**Validation Method:** NABBA interviews

**Success Criteria:** 70%+ of schools confirm this addresses a real pain point

---

### H2: Complement vs. Replace

**Hypothesis:** Schools will adopt ASCYN PRO as a complement to existing systems (Pivot Point, Milady, CourseKey) rather than requiring replacement.

**Current Status:** UNVALIDATED

**Validation Method:** NABBA interviews

**Success Criteria:** 50%+ express interest in complementary adoption

---

### H3: Licensure-Specific Differentiation

**Hypothesis:** Focusing specifically on licensure exam preparation (vs. generic student success) provides meaningful differentiation from CourseKey-style retention platforms.

**Current Status:** UNVALIDATED

**Validation Method:** NABBA interviews

**Success Criteria:** Schools articulate distinction between retention and exam-readiness

---

### H4: Evidence of Improvement Requirement

**Hypothesis:** Schools need documented evidence that remediation worked, not just that it occurred, for accreditation and accountability purposes.

**Current Status:** UNVALIDATED

**Validation Method:** NABBA interviews

**Success Criteria:** Schools confirm documentation of outcomes is a pain point

---

### H5: Willingness to Pay

**Hypothesis:** Schools will pay $999/year for ASCYN PRO's school license.

**Current Status:** UNVALIDATED

**Validation Method:** NABBA interviews + pilot feedback

**Success Criteria:** 30%+ express willingness to pay at proposed price point

---

### H6: Multi-System Fatigue

**Hypothesis:** Instructors are frustrated by using multiple systems and would value tighter integration.

**Current Status:** UNVALIDATED

**Validation Method:** NABBA interviews

**Success Criteria:** Schools confirm multi-system friction
