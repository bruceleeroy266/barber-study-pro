## Positioning Strategy

### Primary Message

> ASCYN PRO helps schools identify learning gaps, target remediation, monitor progress, and intervene earlier as students prepare for licensure.

**Note:** This is the current working description, not necessarily the final NABBA positioning.

---

## What We Will Demonstrate

### Isabella Demo Flow

The existing Isabella demo will demonstrate:

```
Learning Gap
    ↓
Targeted Review
    ↓
New Assessment/Session Result
    ↓
Instructor Visibility
    ↓
Instructor Intervention Documentation
```

**Critical Guardrail:** Do not manufacture capabilities in the demo that production architecture does not currently support.

---

## What We Will NOT Claim

### Claims to Avoid

| Claim | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| "Unique exam blueprint alignment" | Milady ExamReady already provides this |
| "Predicts licensing exam pass probability" | No outcome dataset to support |
| "Replaces your existing curriculum" | Positioning as complement, not replacement |
| "Only system with weak-area identification" | Pivot Point already provides this |
| "Only system with intervention alerts" | CourseKey already provides this |

---

## Key Differentiation Emphasis

### Focus Area

The **instructor-centered workflow after a learning gap is identified**:

1. **Who needs my attention?**
2. **What exactly are they struggling with?**
3. **What should happen next?**
4. **What intervention occurred?**
5. **Did the student improve afterward?**

---

## Validation Questions

### For School Owners

1. How do you currently identify students who are not prepared for their licensing theory exam?
2. What happens after you identify a learning gap?
3. Who is responsible for intervening?
4. How is intervention documented?
5. How do you determine whether remediation worked?
6. What does your existing system fail to show you?
7. Would tighter connection between licensing-exam content and instructor intervention be useful?
8. What would make a system like ASCYN PRO worth paying for?

### For Instructors

1. How do you know which students need help before the licensing exam?
2. What do you do when you identify a struggling student?
3. How do you track whether your intervention worked?
4. Do you have to use multiple systems to manage student progress?
5. What would make your job easier in preparing students for licensure?

## Objection Handling

### Anticipated Objections

| Objection | Response Strategy |
|-----------|-------------------|
| "We already use Pivot Point/Milady" | Position as complement, not replacement |
| "CourseKey already does intervention" | Emphasize licensure-specific remediation |
| "How is this different from ExamReady?" | Focus on instructor workflow, not just alignment |
| "We can't afford another system" | Emphasize complementarity and targeted value |
