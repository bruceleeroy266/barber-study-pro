---

## What Is an Exam Blueprint?

An exam blueprint (also called a content outline or test specification) defines:

- **Domains:** Major content areas covered on the exam
- **Domain weights:** Percentage or number of questions per domain
- **Sub-topics:** Specific topics within each domain
- **Question types:** Multiple choice, etc.

---

## Blueprint vs. CIB

- **CIB** (Candidate Information Bulletin): The full document including policies, procedures, and the blueprint
- **Blueprint**: The content outline portion of the CIB

The blueprint is typically a section within the CIB.
