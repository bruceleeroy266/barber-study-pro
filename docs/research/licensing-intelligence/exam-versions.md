## Purpose

Track exam versions over time. Licensing exams are updated periodically, and historical versions must be preserved for accuracy.

---

## Versioning Chain

```
Exam
  └── Exam Version (e.g., "NIC Barber Theory v2024")
        └── Effective Date
              └── CIB (Candidate Information Bulletin)
                    └── Blueprint (Content Outline)
```

## Why Version Tracking Matters

- Students may be preparing for different exam versions depending on when they test
- Curriculum aligned to an outdated blueprint may not cover current exam content
- Historical data (e.g., "what was on the 2023 exam?") may be needed for research
- States may transition between exam developers, creating version gaps
- California's blueprint update (2026-04-01) demonstrates that blueprint versions change independently of CIB versions
