## Overview

NIC develops standardized examinations for cosmetology and barbering licensure used by multiple states. NIC examinations are administered through various authorized providers.

## Candidate Information Bulletins

NIC publishes CIBs for each examination program. CIBs include:
- Exam content outline
- Domain weights
- Sample questions
- Reference materials
- Exam policies

**Versioning:** CIBs have explicit effective/revision dates. Track versions carefully.

---

## ASCYN PRO Implication

**Key Finding:** NIC is an exam **developer**, not necessarily the administrator.

When researching a state:
1. Identify if NIC develops the exam
2. Identify who administers the exam (may be different)
3. Obtain the current CIB and version
4. Track effective dates

**Do NOT assume:** `exam_provider = NIC` without distinguishing developer vs. administrator.
