## Important Finding: Multiple Knowledge Layers

Licensure readiness in Mississippi requires:

```
Mississippi Barber License
├── NIC Barber Theory (national technical knowledge)
├── Mississippi Law & Sanitation Examination (state-specific regulatory knowledge)
└── Mississippi Barber Practical Examination (practical skills)
```

**Do NOT treat national theory performance alone as complete Mississippi licensure readiness.**
