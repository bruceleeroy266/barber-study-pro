## Important Finding — Early Testing Threshold

South Carolina permits qualifying students to test after completing:

**90% of required program hours**

even though all required hours must still be completed before licensure.

---

## Architecture / Research Implication

Preserve a conceptual distinction between:

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Exam Eligibility Threshold** | 90% program completion — eligible to test |
| **Program Completion / Licensure Threshold** | 100% program completion — eligible for license |
