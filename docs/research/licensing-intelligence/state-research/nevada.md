## Important Finding: Oral Examinations Exist

Nevada requires an **oral examination** component. This adds a new examination type to the licensing-research taxonomy.

**Exam types must not be limited conceptually to `Theory + Practical`.**

National licensing research now supports at least:
- Theory / Written
- Practical
- State Law / Jurisprudence
- Oral
- Proficiency
- Service-specific certification examinations

## ASCYN PRO Implication

Nevada's three-component examination (written + practical + oral) is unique among states researched so far. The oral examination component would require a different preparation approach than written or practical exams. ASCYN PRO would need to consider oral examination preparation if expanding to Nevada.
