## Important Finding: Partial National Program Participation

Massachusetts participates in PSI's National Barber & Cosmetology testing program for the **theory portion only**.

**Do NOT infer:** `PSI National Program = Theory + Practical` for every participating state.

A state may use only a portion of a national testing program.
