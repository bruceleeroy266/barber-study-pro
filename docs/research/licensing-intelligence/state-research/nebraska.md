## Important Finding: Profession-Specific Examination Systems

Nebraska barbering and Nebraska cosmetology do NOT share the same examination structure.

**Nebraska cosmetology examination arrangements must NOT be used as evidence for Nebraska barbering.**

**Architecture Implication:** Never research only `State → Exam`. The minimum safe relationship is:

```
State → Profession → License → Exam Requirement
```

## ASCYN PRO Implication

Nebraska operates its own state barber examination through the Board of Barber Examiners. The Board publishes practical examination instructions and conducts examinations. This is another state-controlled examination model, distinct from NIC or PSI national programs.
