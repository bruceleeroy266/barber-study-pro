## Important Finding: Administrator ≠ Exam Developer

**PSI administers/delivers the examination, but the examination is STATE-SPECIFIC.**

The Indiana Board chose to continue reviewing/updating its existing examination questions rather than adopting the PSI National examination.

**Do NOT infer:** `PSI administrator = PSI National Exam`

Indiana proves that assumption would be incorrect. A jurisdiction may use PSI to administer a state-developed/state-specific examination.

## ASCYN PRO Implication

Indiana is a critical counter-example to the assumption that PSI administration means PSI national exam content. This finding reinforces that exam developer and exam administrator must remain separate concepts. A state-specific exam means ASCYN PRO cannot rely solely on national exam blueprints for curriculum alignment in Indiana.
