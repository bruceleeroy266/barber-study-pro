## Early Testing Eligibility

South Dakota permits qualifying students to become eligible for the theory examination while still enrolled, although all education and practical requirements must be completed before licensure.

| Threshold | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Exam Eligibility** | While still enrolled (qualifying students) |
| **Licensure Eligibility** | All education and practical requirements completed |

---

## Architecture / Research Implication

Preserve the distinction between:

- **Eligibility to Test**
- **Eligibility for Licensure**

South Dakota also reinforces:

**National Theory + State/Board Practical**

as a valid licensing pattern.
