## ASCYN PRO Implication

New Hampshire demonstrates that license subtype matters. Barber and Master Barber use different NIC examination tracks (Barber 1 vs. Barber Styling). ASCYN PRO would need to track which license type a student is pursuing to provide correct exam preparation. The administrator transition (DL Roope/Prometric → Prov) reinforces the need for historical vendor tracking.
