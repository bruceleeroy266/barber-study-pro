## Purpose

Track Candidate Information Bulletins (CIBs) and Test Taker Guides by state and exam. CIBs are authoritative documents that describe exam content, structure, and policies.

---

## What Is a CIB?

A Candidate Information Bulletin is an official document published by an exam developer or administrator that provides:

- Exam content outline / domains
- Number of questions
- Time limits
- Passing standards (if published)
- Exam policies and procedures
- Reference materials

---

## Versioning Requirement

CIBs are updated periodically. Each version must be tracked:

```
Exam → Exam Version → Effective Date → CIB → Blueprint
```

**Do not overwrite historical findings when a newer CIB appears.** Preserve history.

## Reverification

CIBs should be re-verified periodically (recommended: quarterly) because:

- Exam content outlines change
- New CIB versions are published
- Effective dates may shift
- States may change exam developers or administrators
