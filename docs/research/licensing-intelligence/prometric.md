## ASCYN PRO Implication

**Key Finding:** Prometric is an administrator, not a developer.

When researching a state:
1. Identify if Prometric administers the exam
2. Identify who develops the exam content
3. Obtain the developer's CIB and version
4. Track the administration relationship
