## Purpose

Provide a national-level summary of barber licensing examination structures across the United States.

---

## Key Finding: Fragmented Administration

**Confidence:** CONFIRMED  
**Date Verified:** 2026-08-14

Barber licensing examinations in the United States are administered through a fragmented system. No single national exam exists. Each state determines:

- Whether a theory (written) exam is required
- Whether a practical exam is required
- Which organization develops the exam content
- Which organization administers the exam
- Passing standards

---

## Exam Developers vs. Administrators

**This distinction is critical and must not be collapsed.**

| Role | Definition | Examples |
|------|------------|----------|
| **Exam Developer** | Organization responsible for examination content | NIC, PSI |
| **Exam Administrator** | Organization responsible for delivering the examination | Prov, PSI, Prometric, DL Roope, PCS, state agencies |

A single organization may serve both roles in some jurisdictions (e.g., PSI develops and administers some exams). In other jurisdictions, the roles are split (e.g., Oklahoma uses NIC content administered through Prov).

---

## Known Exam Organizations

| Organization | Role(s) | Notes |
|-------------|---------|-------|
| **NIC** (National-Interstate Council) | Exam Developer | Publishes CIBs and exam blueprints; content used by multiple states |
| **PSI** | Developer + Administrator | May serve different roles depending on jurisdiction; has PSI-developed national exam programs |
| **Prov** | Administrator | Administers exams in multiple states; does not develop content |
| **Prometric** | Administrator | Administers exams in some jurisdictions |
| **DL Roope** | Administrator | Administers exams in some jurisdictions |
| **PCS** | Administrator | Administers exams in some jurisdictions |
| **State Agencies/Boards** | Administrator | Some states administer their own exams |

---

## Initial Research Findings

- Jurisdictions may use organizations including Prov, PSI, Prometric, DL Roope, PCS, and state agencies/boards.
- Do not infer the actual exam blueprint solely from the administrator.
- NIC publishes examination/Candidate Information Bulletin information and exam blueprints.
- PSI may serve different roles depending on jurisdiction.
