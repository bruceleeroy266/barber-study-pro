## Overview

DL Roope is an examination administration provider that delivers licensing examinations, including NIC examinations for barbering and cosmetology.

## ASCYN PRO Implication

**Key Finding:** DL Roope is an administrator, not a developer.

When researching a state:
1. Identify if DL Roope administers the exam
2. Identify who develops the exam content (typically NIC)
3. Obtain the developer's CIB and version
4. Track the administration relationship
