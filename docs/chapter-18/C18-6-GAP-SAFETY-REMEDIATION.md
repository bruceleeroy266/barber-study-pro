# C18-6 — Gap Detection + Safety Escalation + Targeted Remediation

C18-6 turns the certified Chapter 18 evidence architecture into actionable gap detection and targeted remediation.

## Preserved inventory

C18-6 does not rewrite or expand the certified learning content:

- 1 real runtime lesson shell
- 10 logical lesson sections
- 50 certified flashcards
- 15 certified assessment questions
- 14 certified micro-check questions
- 7 canonical concept families
- immutable first-attempt micro-check evidence
- shared 20/10/40/15/15 grading architecture

## Combined evidence

Chapter 18 can now combine concept evidence from:

- micro-checks
- flashcards
- chapter assessment
- scenario/application evidence when such evidence exists

Duplicate evidence with the same student/chapter/source/phase/item identity is ignored instead of counted twice.

The original immutable evidence array is retained in the remediation plan.

## Ordinary concept-gap targeting

A concept is targeted when combined evidence meets either of these conditions:

- at least 2 evidence observations and mastery at or below 70; or
- at least 2 preserved initial misses.

Ordinary remediation uses:

- the real `chapter-18-lesson` runtime shell;
- only the flashcards canonically mapped to the weak concept;
- a planned 5-question reassessment;
- an 80% recovery threshold.

C18-6 plans the reassessment contract but does not create the fresh reassessment reserve. That is C18-7.

## Real-shell remediation architecture

Chapter 18 still has one runtime HTML lesson block. C18-6 therefore maps all seven concepts back to that real block for remediation rather than inventing fake runtime section IDs.

Concept specificity comes from:

- the C18-1 semantic 10-section map;
- concept-specific flashcards;
- concept evidence and priority.

## Chapter 18 safety model

The safety model is derived from the hardened haircolor/lightening content and is intentionally different from Chapter 17.

Exactly four high-risk hazard classes are defined:

1. **allergy_scalp_contraindication**
   - product-directed allergy-alert/skin-test boundaries
   - visible irritated, sunburned, or compromised scalp

2. **product_use_handling_boundary**
   - off-scalp-only vs on-scalp restrictions
   - facial-hair use only when expressly permitted
   - damaged/compromised chemical packaging
   - manufacturer-defined use/handling limits

3. **compatibility_unknown_history**
   - unknown prior home color or chemical history
   - progressive/metallic-salt compatibility concerns
   - no improvised chemical combinations

4. **overlap_overprocessing_control**
   - lightener retouch placement
   - previously lightened/sensitized hair
   - developer/timing/heat/overlap limits

## Escalation rules

A single tagged safety miss triggers immediate **targeted safety review** and instructor visibility.

It does not by itself trigger urgent formal reassessment.

Urgent escalation requires, within the five most recent tagged observations:

- at least 2 distinct missed safety items; and
- at least 2 distinct hazard classes.

This prevents two misses from the same hazard from being treated as a broader multi-hazard safety breakdown.

Urgent policy:

- targeted review: required
- instructor review: required
- formal safety reassessment: required
- question count: 5
- required score: 100%

Only concepts involved in the urgent safety evidence receive the 100% requirement. Ordinary concepts remain at 80%.

## Clearing safety intervention

An active intervention clears only after five consecutive correct tagged high-risk observations.

The historical misses remain evidence; clearing the active intervention does not erase diagnostic history.

## Student-facing safety language

Student messages are operational and educational, not diagnostic or medical.

The safety layer specifically rejects language that:

- diagnoses infection/disease/allergic reaction;
- claims a client “has” a condition;
- prescribes treatment.

## Shared registries

C18-6 registers Chapter 18 with:

- the shared concept-detection registry;
- the shared remediation-assignment registry;
- the shared remediation-content provider.

Every canonical concept receives:

- the real Chapter 18 lesson shell as a content assignment;
- one or more concept-specific flashcards.

## Live micro-check integration

A tagged wrong micro-check answer immediately displays a **Targeted Safety Review** message after the immutable first attempt is saved.

The student cannot edit or overwrite the original answer.

## C18-7 boundary

C18-6 intentionally does **not** create reassessment questions.

C18-7 will create exactly five fresh reassessment questions per canonical concept family, separate from the 15-question assessment and 14 micro-check namespaces, and will implement mastery recovery without erasing original misses.
