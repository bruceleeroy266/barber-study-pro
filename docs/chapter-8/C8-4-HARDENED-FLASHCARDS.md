# C8-4 — Hardened 50-Card Baseline & Coverage Recheck

## Result

All 39 non-KEEP Chapter 8 flashcards have now been rewritten or replaced in place.

The original 50-card structure is preserved:

- **50 active cards**
- **50 unique existing IDs**
- **11 audited KEEP cards remain in their original conceptual slots**
- **31 audited REWRITE cards hardened**
- **8 audited REPLACE cards replaced with new ASCYN PRO wording**
- **0 IDs deleted**
- **0 order positions deleted**

## Major repairs completed

The hardened deck now removes or corrects:

- "BOARD EXAM ALERT" certainty
- claims that concepts appear on every state board exam
- converter/rectifier confusion
- universal GFCI legal wording
- "water turns insulators into conductors"
- fatality/legal/negligence language
- universal 80% breaker-loading guidance
- advice to redesign or redistribute facility circuits
- universal galvanic contraindication lists
- absolute pacemaker/liability claims
- guaranteed galvanic/microcurrent/high-frequency outcomes
- fixed infrared/light-device operating distances
- universal five-minute light-therapy exposure limits
- guaranteed LED treatment outcomes
- universal laser authorization assumptions

## What the deck now emphasizes

- source-grounded electrical terminology
- equipment ratings and stop-use conditions
- grounding and ground-fault protection by function
- manufacturer/device instructions
- scope of practice
- client screening
- continuous supervision
- application/safety reasoning instead of slogans
- source-described modality effects rather than guaranteed medical outcomes

## Expansion decision

**Yes — targeted expansion is warranted.**

The 50-card baseline is now materially safer and more accurate, but two canonical concept families remain too thin to support the Chapter 7+ evidence model:

1. **Microcurrent & High-Frequency Modalities — 2 cards**
2. **Light-Therapy Safety — 1 card**

No broad expansion is justified for the already-dense equipment-safety or galvanic families.

The next expansion should add only enough new original ASCYN PRO cards to give those two thin families multiple independent evidence points, with emphasis on application/scenario reasoning rather than additional recall.

## Expansion boundary

Before adding cards, the hardened 50-card baseline must pass:

- TypeScript
- lint
- unit/integrity tests
- production build
- Pilot Onboarding Certification
- exact-head Vercel deployment

Once GREEN, add targeted cards for the two thin concept families and update mappings/tests accordingly.
