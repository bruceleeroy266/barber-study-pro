# C8-4 — Flashcard Hardening & Coverage Expansion

## Phase rule

Before changing card wording, every existing active Chapter 8 flashcard is classified:

- **KEEP** — concept and wording are acceptable with no material safety/accuracy change required.
- **REWRITE** — the concept should remain, but wording must be hardened, clarified, de-overclaimed, or made more application-oriented.
- **REPLACE** — the current card's premise is too unsafe, legally overbroad, misleading, or structurally weak to preserve; replace it with a new original ASCYN PRO card for the same concept family.

The baseline remains **50 active cards** until all 50 are audited.

## Highest-priority REPLACE cards

### fc-8-017 — GFCI legal claim
Current problem:
- states GFCI is universally “REQUIRED near sinks and all water sources.”

Replacement goal:
- teach what ground-fault protection does;
- direct the learner to applicable code/facility requirements;
- preserve water-exposure safety without inventing universal law.

### fc-8-023 — circuit-loading rule
Current problem:
- presents an 80% breaker rule as a universal barber calculation;
- tells the learner to spread tools across multiple circuits.

Replacement goal:
- read equipment ratings;
- avoid overloaded receptacles/power strips;
- treat repeated trips, heat, odor, or damaged connections as stop/escalate conditions;
- leave facility electrical design to qualified personnel.

### fc-8-032 — galvanic contraindications
Current problem:
- universal contraindication list presented as absolute.

Replacement goal:
- client screening;
- device/manufacturer contraindications;
- scope and training;
- stop when the client's condition or device instructions make the service inappropriate.

### fc-8-041 — light-therapy exposure
Current problem:
- universal five-minute maximum.

Replacement goal:
- use device-specific exposure time;
- required eye protection;
- client supervision;
- stop-service symptoms.

### fc-8-044 — light-therapy safety
Current problem:
- fixed 30–36 inch distance;
- five-minute universal limit;
- improvised “wave hand through light path” rule.

Replacement goal:
- device-specific operating distance/time;
- manufacturer-required eye protection;
- medication/photosensitivity/device contraindication screening;
- continuous supervision.

### fc-8-049 — pacemaker/metal implant
Current problem:
- absolute contraindication framing;
- legal/liability conclusion.

Replacement goal:
- treat implanted electronic devices and other device-listed contraindications as high-priority screening conditions;
- do not proceed unless the specific equipment instructions, training, and scope support the service.

### fc-8-050 — “every state board exam”
Current problem:
- claims 25 concepts appear on every exam;
- compresses multiple overclaims into one answer.

Replacement goal:
- neutral Chapter 8 high-value concept synthesis;
- no prediction of exact state-board questions;
- reinforce safety/application reasoning rather than memorizing slogans.

## REWRITE themes

The 33 rewrite cards fall into these major repair groups:

1. Remove “BOARD EXAM ALERT” certainty.
2. Correct converter versus rectifier language.
3. Add supply/device context to voltage/current statements.
4. Remove legal/negligence conclusions.
5. Replace absolute grounding/GFCI language with purpose and applicable-requirement language.
6. Present galvanic pole effects as source-described modality concepts rather than guaranteed physiology.
7. Replace universal electrotherapy treatment sequences with device/product/procedure boundaries.
8. Qualify microcurrent/high-frequency claims as source-described/device-dependent.
9. Remove universal wavelength/penetration shortcuts.
10. Prevent UVC/germicidal language from implying required barber disinfection can be replaced.
11. Remove fixed infrared/light-device distances.
12. Qualify LED/therapeutic-lamp claims.
13. Add laser authorization/scope boundaries.

## Coverage expansion principle

C8-2 showed that the deck is thin in:
- microcurrent/high-frequency,
- light-therapy safety.

C8-4 will first harden all 50 existing cards **without reducing count**.

After the 50-card deck is GREEN, additional cards may be added only if needed to close documented concept/evidence gaps. Expansion is not a substitute for repairing weak legacy cards.

## C8-4 sequence

1. Lock 50-card KEEP / REWRITE / REPLACE registry.
2. Rewrite/replace all non-KEEP cards in original ASCYN PRO wording.
3. Preserve all IDs where the same conceptual slot remains useful.
4. Re-run concept coverage counts.
5. Add new cards only for remaining thin concepts.
6. Run adversarial wording/safety review.
7. Engineering Verification + Vercel exact-head validation.
