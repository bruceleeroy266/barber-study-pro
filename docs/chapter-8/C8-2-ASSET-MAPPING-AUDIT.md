# C8-2 — Existing Asset Mapping & Coverage Audit

## Purpose

Map the current Chapter 8 lesson, flashcards, and assessment into the ten canonical concept families **before any content rewriting**.

This phase records what the current assets actually cover. It does not certify their accuracy.

## Exact current inventory

The audit corrected an earlier rough count.

Current Chapter 8 premium assets contain:

- **31 top-level lesson sections**
- **50 active flashcards**
- **30 assessment questions**

The Chapter 8 premium flashcard file does **not** currently contain ~80 active flashcards.

## Mapping rules

- Every current top-level lesson section must map to at least one canonical Chapter 8 concept.
- Cross-cutting lesson/review blocks may map to multiple concepts when their content genuinely spans multiple families.
- Every active flashcard receives exactly one primary concept mapping.
- Every current assessment question receives exactly one primary concept mapping.
- Mapping means “this asset currently attempts to assess/teach this concept.” It does **not** mean the asset is accurate, safe, or ready to keep.

## Coverage findings

### Over-concentrated areas

The current bank is heavily concentrated in:

- equipment safety,
- electrical measurements,
- galvanic/electrotherapy facts.

That concentration is not inherently wrong, but it means the current 30-question assessment does not yet reflect the balanced, difficult application-oriented framework used in Chapter 7.

### Under-covered areas

The weakest assessment coverage is:

- electrotherapy terminology — 1 question,
- microcurrent/high-frequency — 1 question,
- light-therapy safety — 1 question.

The flashcard deck is especially weak for:

- microcurrent/high-frequency — 2 cards,
- light-therapy safety — 1 card.

Those families need deliberate strengthening during hardening.

## Accuracy/safety audit flags

The mapping exercise confirms several existing assets should be reviewed rather than automatically preserved.

Examples include current items asserting or implying:

- a universal GFCI requirement without jurisdiction/context boundaries,
- a fixed maximum light-therapy exposure time,
- categorical contraindication lists,
- specific therapeutic outcomes for LED colors, UV, infrared, microcurrent, and high-frequency current,
- universal pole/product selection rules,
- a claim that certain concepts appear on “every state board exam,”
- “BOARD EXAM ALERT” language that may overstate exam certainty,
- fixed voltage danger or injury thresholds without appropriate context,
- legal/liability conclusions.

These are mapping findings only. No wording is changed in C8-2.

## Assessment-quality findings

The current 30-question assessment is:

- 10 easy,
- 10 medium,
- 10 hard,
- substantially recall/definition driven,
- less scenario-heavy than the certified Chapter 7 assessment,
- too small to support the intended concept breadth at Chapter 7 quality.

Later assessment hardening should rebuild the bank using original ASCYN PRO wording, same-family distractors, safety/procedure logic, application/scenario emphasis, and explicit concept coverage targets.

## Lesson findings

All 31 top-level sections are now accounted for.

Several lesson sections are cross-cutting review or motivational sections rather than clean instructional units:

- `why-electricity-matters`
- `electrical-certification`
- `memory-aids`
- `board-exam-alerts`
- `hazard-scenarios`
- `action-prompts`
- `power-pledge`

Their mappings are intentionally broader. During lesson hardening, these sections should be evaluated for instructional value, duplication, unsupported claims, and whether they should remain separate sections.

## C8-2 exit criteria

C8-2 closes only when:

- all 31 current top-level lesson sections are mapped;
- all 50 active flashcards are mapped exactly once;
- all 30 assessment questions are mapped exactly once;
- no unknown concept IDs exist;
- every canonical family has at least one lesson mapping and at least one flashcard/assessment evidence item;
- coverage concentrations and gaps are documented;
- accuracy/safety risks are documented without rewriting content;
- Engineering Verification passes;
- Vercel is READY on the exact C8-2 head.

After C8-2, begin **C8-3 — Lesson Content Hardening**, using the Chapter 8 source pages and current NIC scope to rewrite unsafe, overstated, outdated, or weak lesson content in original ASCYN PRO language before touching the flashcards or assessment.
