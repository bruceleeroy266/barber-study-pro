# C17-4 — Assessment Source-Grounding & Answer-Key Certification

C17-4 independently audits and hardens the existing 30-question Chapter 17 chapter assessment against the certified C17-2 lesson while preserving the C17-1 architecture.

## Source boundary

The repository still does not contain a `textbook-images/chapter-17` source folder, so C17-4 does **not** claim page-by-page textbook verification.

The governing baseline is:

- the hardened C17-2 Chapter 17 lesson;
- the certified C17-3 flashcard chemistry/safety boundaries as a consistency cross-check;
- the existing Chapter 17 competency and learning-objective traceability;
- the C17-1 canonical concept mappings;
- conservative manufacturer-direction boundaries for chemical timing, strength, heat, compatibility, rinsing, neutralization, and aftercare.

No textbook prose is copied.

## Answer-key certification

Every one of the 30 chapter-assessment questions is checked for:

- a valid `a/b/c/d` answer key
- four non-empty choices
- four distinct choices
- a non-empty keyed answer
- a non-empty explanation
- concept-family coverage

## Chemistry repairs

C17-4 removes or repairs:

- the claim that all chemical texture services use one identical break-and-rebuild disulfide cycle;
- hydroxide relaxers described as simply breaking disulfide bonds like thio chemistry;
- neutralization wording that could be applied to hydroxide systems;
- underprocessing/overprocessing explanations that overstate one universal bond mechanism.

The assessment now distinguishes:

- thio/permanent-wave reduction and oxidation;
- hydroxide lanthionization and separate post-service finishing requirements.

## Manufacturer-dependent claims hardened

Removed or narrowed:

- fixed alkaline-wave pH range
- blanket acid-wave heat requirement
- fixed “three test curls” rule
- generic “milder acid formula” recommendation for highlighted hair
- porosity as the sole determinant of processing time
- generic timing/product-strength assumptions

Questions now defer timing, heat, product selection, test-curl placement, and service suitability to the complete analysis plus the specific product/manufacturer directions.

## Compatibility and safety hardening

C17-4 now:

- treats hydroxide/thio history as a known incompatibility unless verified product-system guidance explicitly supports the planned service;
- states that a strand test does not override a known incompatibility;
- removes automatic “thio relaxer → thio perm is compatible if healthy” logic;
- requires verification of exact prior chemistry, fiber condition, and manufacturer compatibility guidance;
- postpones service for visibly open, abraded, irritated, or otherwise compromised scalp tissue without diagnosing disease;
- directs burning/pain responses to stop the service and remove product according to safety directions.

## Texturizer hardening

The assessment no longer defines a texturizer only as:

- a “mild” relaxer formula, or
- shorter processing time.

It tests the intended outcome: loosening or softening curl rather than maximizing straightening, with procedure controlled by the selected product system.

## Exam-certainty hardening

Internal assessment comments were changed from:

- “30 board-style questions”
- “BOARD-STYLE QUESTION BANK”

to neutral chapter-assessment wording.

No claim is made that these exact questions are guaranteed licensing-exam content.
