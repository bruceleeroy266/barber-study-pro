## Final invariants

C19-9 must prove all of the following without weakening prior certified behavior:

1. The seven canonical Chapter 19 concept families remain stable.
2. Every concept has lesson, flashcard, assessment, micro-check, remediation, and reassessment coverage.
3. Assessment, micro-check, and reassessment namespaces remain separate.
4. Reassessment never erases or rewrites original diagnostic evidence.
5. Ordinary remediation requires a five-question reassessment with 80% passing.
6. Licensing/employment-law compliance remediation remains separate from bodily safety and requires five questions at 80%.
7. Urgent practical-safety recovery is limited to `ch19-practical-exam-safety-readiness` and requires 5/5 = 100%.
8. Canonical remediation uses the real `chapter-19-lesson` shell and mapped flashcards, not stale legacy `CH19-R-qq-19-*` or `CH19-R-LO1/2/3` routes.
9. Instructor and school-admin diagnostics remain behind the existing role and same-school authorization boundary.
10. Diagnostic UI does not expose raw answer payloads, student IDs, question IDs, item IDs, or remediation-cycle IDs.
11. Shared grading weights remain 20/10/40/15/15.

This is an integrity constraint, not a waiver of the shared grading architecture.
