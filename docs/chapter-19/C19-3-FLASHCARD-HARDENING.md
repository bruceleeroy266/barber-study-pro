## Licensing and exam repairs

The inherited deck contained several overbroad exam claims.

Repaired examples include:

- no universal claim that barber licensure requires exactly one written and one practical exam;
- no universal list of practical-exam technical skills;
- no assumption that every practical exam uses a live model or mannequin in the same way;
- no generic practical-exam kit rule;
- no assumption that another jurisdiction's timing, procedures, scoring, or supplies apply;
- accommodations requests are routed to the current authorized provider or licensing agency;
- state-law cards explicitly recognize jurisdictional variation and change over time.

## Test-taking strategy repairs

C19-3 removes brittle heuristics that could teach the wrong rule.

Examples:

- absolute words such as “always” and “never” are no longer taught as usually false by default;
- “answer easy questions first” is now conditional on the testing system permitting navigation;
- skipped questions are returned to only when the testing system and instructions allow it;
- exam directions control matching, essay, and navigation behavior.

## Employment-readiness and résumé repairs

The deck no longer teaches unsupported universal formulas such as:

- employers scan every résumé for exactly 20 seconds;
- one fixed list of résumé contents;
- a cover letter belongs inside the résumé;
- all employers require the same application process.

Cards now emphasize:

- accurate current credentials only when actually earned;
- relevant accomplishments and experience;
- accessible portfolio formats appropriate to the employer;
- employer-specific application instructions.

## Interview and follow-up repairs

Removed arbitrary timing/formula claims such as:

- always follow up exactly one week after submitting a résumé;
- interview answers should always be two minutes or less;
- students need exactly two interview outfits.

The hardened cards now teach professional preparation, clear communication, relevant follow-up, and employer-specific instructions without inventing universal timing rules.

## Legal and contract repairs

Cards 056–059 were materially hardened.

C19-3 no longer teaches:

- one universal list of illegal interview-question topics;
- one exact work-authorization sentence as universally legal;
- blanket assumptions about noncompete enforceability.

The cards now state that:

- interview-law rules and protected categories vary by jurisdiction and situation;
- work-authorization wording and legal boundaries depend on applicable law and hiring procedures;
- contract meaning and enforceability depend on wording and applicable law;
- significant agreements should be read completely and reviewed with qualified legal help when needed.
