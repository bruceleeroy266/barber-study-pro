## Verdict target

C19-2 hardens the active Chapter 19 lesson against unsupported universal licensing, examination, worker-classification, interview-law, and contract claims while preserving the C19-1 seven-family architecture and ASCYN provenance rules.

## Source boundary

The active Chapter 19 learner lesson is original ASCYN PRO supplemental content. C19-2 does not claim that the repository contains a controlling legal source for every U.S. jurisdiction, licensing board, examination provider, employment relationship, or contract provision.

Therefore C19-2 applies a strict boundary:

- licensing and examination details are jurisdiction- and provider-specific;
- official licensing agencies and authorized examination providers control current exam/application instructions;
- accommodations processes and deadlines must be checked with the applicable official source;
- worker classification, compensation obligations, benefits, taxes, and contract enforceability are not inferred from labels alone;
- interview-law and protected-category rules are not presented as one universal list;
- legal or employment questions with material consequences are routed to appropriate official or qualified professional sources;
- no publisher-branded textbook wording or page citations are introduced.

## Hardening repairs

### Licensing requirements

Replaced broad wording that implied one universal pre-work sequence with a narrower rule:

- verify the specific license and jurisdiction;
- verify current education/training eligibility, examination components, application steps, fees, identification, and documentation;
- distinguish performing work that legally requires a license from general employment preparation;
- use the applicable official licensing agency and authorized exam provider.

### Testing accommodations

Removed any implied universal documentation/approval process.

The lesson now requires the student to verify:

- the current request process;
- documentation rules;
- deadlines;

with the authorized testing provider or licensing agency.

### Practical / skills examination

Removed the phrase that treated one generic candidate guide as a universal “source of truth.”

The hardened lesson now:

- requires current instructions from the applicable official agency/provider;
- explicitly warns that another jurisdiction’s procedure list, timing, kit, scoring, or sequence may not apply;
- treats infection control, client protection, tool handling, and safety as procedural requirements when the official instructions require them;
- requires verification of permitted or required kit, supplies, labeling, and setup rules.

### Employment readiness and credentials

Narrowed “employment readiness begins before graduation” to “can begin before graduation.”

The résumé section now prevents students from implying that a license or credential has already been earned when it has not.

### Shop research, compensation, and worker classification

Removed wording that could imply that an arrangement is legally resolved by calling it employee, commission, booth rental, or another label.

The lesson now teaches that:

- labels alone do not determine tax, wage, benefit, or worker-classification consequences;
- important terms should be confirmed directly;
- public-facing shop information may be incomplete.

### Interview-law wording

Removed the broad statement that questions about protected characteristics are categorically “restricted or inappropriate” everywhere.

The hardened lesson now states:

- interview-question rules and protected categories can vary by jurisdiction and situation;
- a generic “legal/illegal question” list is not universal law;
- concerning situations should be verified through a qualified workforce or legal resource.

### Agreements, noncompete, confidentiality, and compensation terms

The lesson now states that meaning and enforceability can depend on:

- the actual wording;
- applicable law;
- the facts of the working relationship.

It specifically avoids teaching that a clause is automatically valid or invalid everywhere.
