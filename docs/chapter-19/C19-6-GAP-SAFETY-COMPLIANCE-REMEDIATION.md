## Ordinary concept-gap targeting

A canonical Chapter 19 concept becomes an ordinary remediation target when either:

- at least 2 observations exist and concept mastery is at or below 70%; or
- at least 2 preserved initial misses exist.

Ordinary remediation uses:

- the real `chapter-19-lesson` shell;
- only flashcards canonically mapped to the weak concept;
- a planned 5-question reassessment;
- an 80% recovery threshold.

## Practical-safety path

Only the canonical family:

`ch19-practical-exam-safety-readiness`

is eligible for bodily-safety escalation.

C19-6 defines two practical-safety hazard classes:

1. `infection_control_omission`
2. `practical_service_safety_process`

A single tagged miss triggers immediate targeted safety review and instructor visibility.

Urgent safety escalation requires, within the five most recent tagged safety observations:

- at least 2 distinct missed tagged items; and
- both practical-safety hazard classes.

Urgent recovery policy:

- targeted safety review: required;
- instructor review: required;
- formal reassessment: required;
- reassessment size: 5 questions;
- required score: 100%.

The 100% rule applies only to the affected practical-safety concept.

## Distinct compliance/legal path

Licensing and employment-law issues remain separate from bodily safety.

Compliance/legal-critical families:

- `ch19-licensing-requirements-verification`;
- `ch19-employment-law-contracts-compliance`.

Compliance domains:

1. `licensing_requirements`
2. `employment_law_contracts`

A single tagged compliance miss triggers targeted compliance review and instructor visibility.

Two distinct recent compliance misses trigger an elevated compliance state and planned formal reassessment.

Compliance recovery remains:

- 5 questions;
- 80% threshold.

No licensing, interview-law, worker-classification, agreement, or contract issue is converted into a 100% bodily-safety rule.
