# C4 50-Card Flashcard Book-Alignment Audit — LO1–LO8

## Scope
Primary source: connected Drive `chapter 4.pdf` plus the matching Chapter 4 source artifact used to structure the LO1–LO8 lesson alignment.

This audit makes **no flashcard or quiz changes**. It classifies the current canonical `fc-4-001..050` deck before implementation.

## Baseline result
- Existing cards: **50**
- Recommended RETAIN: **43**
- Recommended REWRITE: **7**
- Recommended supplements: **20**
- Proposed final deck: **70 cards**
- Original IDs `fc-4-001..050` remain stable
- Proposed new IDs: `fc-4-051..070`
- Initial quiz remains **30 questions** and is out of scope for this phase.

The current deck is stronger than the Chapter 5 baseline. Its primary weakness is not factual instability; it is **under-coverage of several source details**, especially bacterial morphology/stages, specific disinfectant categories, handwashing/sanitizer distinctions, and safe-work-practice topics.

### REWRITE — 7 cards

| Card | Rewrite reason | Replacement direction |
|---|---|---|
| fc-4-011 | Current definition is generally correct but omits the source's key limitation. | Teach disinfection as destruction of most pathogenic microorganisms on appropriate nonporous items **except bacterial spores**, used exactly as labeled. |

| fc-4-027 | Generic work-surface card is valid but LO7 has much larger uncovered source material. | Repurpose to ventilation/airborne chemical and dust safety. |
| fc-4-035 | Exposure-control-plan card is broader OSHA enrichment and duplicates regulatory architecture while a source exposure concept is missing. | Rewrite to define an **exposure incident** and connect non-intact skin/blood/body-fluid contact to immediate procedure. |
