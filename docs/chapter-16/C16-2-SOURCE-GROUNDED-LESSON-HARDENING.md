## Source boundary

The current repository does not contain a complete authoritative Milady Chapter 16 textbook source set for this pass.

Available project material supports Chapter 16 as a women's haircutting/styling topic and preserves the existing subject structure, but it does not justify universal licensing-exam frequency claims, universal technique rules, or textbook-figure assertions as if the underlying source pages were currently available for direct verification.

C16-2 therefore follows the same source-boundary discipline used in prior hardened chapters:

- preserve established haircutting subject matter and useful instructional structure;
- remove or narrow unsupported board/licensing certainty;
- narrow safety and procedure absolutes where the repository does not provide authoritative support;
- keep ASCYN PRO wording original;
- do not copy textbook prose;
- defer flashcard and assessment repairs to their own later phases.

## Repairs made

- replaced seven “BOARD ALERT” headings with instructional “CORE CONCEPTS” headings;
- removed unsupported claims that licensing exams expect, frequently test, often test, or consistently test specific Chapter 16 content;
- narrowed client-consultation absolutes;
- narrowed the claim that curly hair must never be treated like straight hair and removed unsupported “curl memory” certainty;
- reframed curl shrinkage as client-variable rather than universal in degree;
- narrowed razor suitability claims to hair condition, texture, density, desired finish, and technique;
- removed the absolute “best for dense hair” texturizing claim;
- narrowed dull-tool and undercut/damage wording;
- replaced unsupported textbook-figure descriptions with ASCYN PRO visual-learning intent;
- narrowed blow-dry and cool-shot claims;
- replaced thermal-tool absolutes with manufacturer-direction, lowest-effective-heat, dry-hair/default-use, and client-protection guidance;
- preserved sanitation/cleanup as professional practice without adding jurisdiction-specific regulatory claims.
