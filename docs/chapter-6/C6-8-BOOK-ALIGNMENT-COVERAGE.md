# C6-8 — Chapter 6 Book Alignment Coverage Map

## Source basis
Primary source: the 24-page Chapter 6 PDF stored in the connected Google Drive as `chapter 6.pdf`.

ASCYN PRO uses the source chapter for **topic coverage, terminology, sequencing, and factual scope**, but rewrites the teaching language, examples, scenarios, explanations, flashcards, and assessment items in original ASCYN PRO wording. No textbook passages are copied into production content.

## Book-supported gaps identified
### Skeletal
ASCYN PRO did not explicitly teach humerus, ulna, radius, carpus/carpals, metacarpals, phalanges, hyoid, or the source chapter's broader chest/shoulder/back framing.

### Muscular
ASCYN PRO under-covered origin/belly/insertion and the source chapter's shoulder/arm/forearm emphasis, including deltoid, biceps, triceps, flexor/extensor, pronator, and supinator.

### Nervous
ASCYN PRO covered CN V and VII generally but did not teach the source chapter's named facial-nerve branches or cervical nerves: temporal, zygomatic, buccal, marginal mandibular, greater occipital, lesser occipital, greater auricular, and cervical cutaneous.

### Cardiovascular
ASCYN PRO covered the carotids, superficial temporal artery, facial artery, and jugular veins, but did not teach the source chapter's detailed named branches: supraorbital, submental, inferior/superior labial, frontal, parietal, transverse facial, middle temporal, anterior auricular, and posterior auricular.

### Blood
The source chapter explicitly reviews functions of blood, including transport, temperature regulation, defense, and clotting. That detail was not adequately represented in the current lesson/assessment.

### Endocrine
The source chapter distinguishes endocrine (ductless) glands from exocrine (duct) glands and defines hormones as secretions that influence body activity. ASCYN PRO should retain this distinction in its own wording.

## Implementation rule
All new material must:
- stay inside the existing 10 Chapter 6 concept families
- be rewritten in original ASCYN PRO wording
- preserve barber relevance without inventing medical claims
- avoid diagnosis/treatment language outside barber scope
- be mapped into lesson, flashcards, assessment, remediation, and instructor diagnostics
- pass the existing C6 adversarial certification plus new book-alignment tests
