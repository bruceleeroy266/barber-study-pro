# C7-3 — Chapter 7 Lesson Content Hardening

## Purpose

Harden the Chapter 7 lesson before changing flashcards or the chapter assessment.

## Source hierarchy

1. Chapter 7 textbook PDF is the coverage, terminology, sequence, and learning-objective reference.
2. NIC Barber Theory CIB is the exam-domain reference for basic chemistry, especially:
   - pH
   - purpose/effects of products and ingredients
   - interactions among chemicals
   - chemical reactions and overexposure safety
3. ASCYN PRO owns the instructional expression. Production wording is rewritten and does not copy textbook sentences, tables, examples, or question wording.

## Textbook coverage verified

The Chapter 7 source covers:

- organic and inorganic chemistry
- matter, elements, atoms, molecules, and compounds
- physical and chemical properties/changes
- oxidation, reduction, redox, exothermic, and endothermic reactions
- pure substances, physical mixtures, and chemical compounds
- solutions, suspensions, emulsions, and surfactants
- water purification, soft/hard water, and pH
- acids, alkalis, and acid-alkali neutralization
- shampoo chemistry, surfactant classifications, and shampoo categories
- conditioner classifications and ingredient families
- styling aids and other cosmetic preparations used in barbering
- selected cosmetic ingredient terminology

## High-risk legacy claims corrected

The hardened lesson removes or rewrites shortcuts that could create false mastery:

- organic does not mean "living or once living"
- organic/inorganic classification does not predict burn, irritation, or flammability by itself
- physical versus chemical change is not defined only by reversible versus permanent
- peroxide/redox language now identifies the oxidized and reduced substances more carefully
- permanent-wave oxidation neutralization is separated from acid-alkali neutralization
- pH teaching distinguishes the chemical pH scale from the normal hair/skin range
- fixed shampoo schedules were removed in favor of matching product purpose and client condition
- universal patch-test/legal claims were removed; required testing now depends on product directions and applicable rules
- "every state board exam" claims were removed
- brand-specific disinfectant claims were removed from the chemistry lesson

## Copyright boundary

ASCYN PRO uses the textbook to identify subject matter, terminology, sequence, and learning objectives. The production lesson uses original explanations, examples, cautions, and exam-prep framing. No textbook sentences, tables, figures, or assessment items are reproduced.

## C7-3 verification

C7-3 tests enforce that:

- all ten canonical concept families remain represented
- removed shortcuts do not return
- neutralization concepts remain separated
- pH terminology remains source-aligned
- runtime lesson copy does not expose the textbook publisher/source
- unsupported universal exam/testing claims do not return

## Exit criteria

C7-3 closes only after:

- hardened lesson wording is committed
- regression tests are committed
- Engineering Verification is GREEN
- Vercel is GREEN

Only then should Chapter 7 flashcards or the assessment be hardened.
