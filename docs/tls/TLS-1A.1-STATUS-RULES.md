# TLS-1A.1 — Strong / Improving / Needs Attention Rules

Status: LOCKED FOR PLANNING — NO RUNTIME CODE CHANGES

## Purpose

Give instructors one understandable status and one recommended action while preserving ASCYN PRO's certified Chapters 1–21 evidence and grading contracts underneath.

This phase does not replace or alter the certified 20/10/40/15/15 grade calculation, remediation thresholds, safety/compliance rules, or diagnostic history.

## Display rules

### Strong
Use **Strong** when the student's current certified mastery result is **80% or higher** and there is **no unresolved required remediation or safety/compliance recovery**.

Recommended action: **Continue learning / maintain mastery.**

### Improving
Use **Improving** when the student has demonstrated successful recovery on the current weakness (including the required recovery threshold) but the preserved evidence shows that the concept required intervention during the current learning cycle.

Recommended action: **Continue targeted practice and monitor the next fresh evidence.**

A recovered concept does not erase the original miss. Improving is the bridge between intervention and sustained Strong performance.

### Needs Attention
Use **Needs Attention** when any of the following is true:
- current certified mastery is **below 80%**;
- required remediation/reassessment remains incomplete or unsuccessful;
- an unresolved urgent safety or compliance requirement is present.

Recommended action: **Target the identified weak concept and complete the required remediation/reassessment.**

## Priority / precedence

When more than one condition exists, the visible status uses the highest intervention need:

1. **Needs Attention** — unresolved weakness, remediation, safety, or compliance requirement.
2. **Improving** — successful recovery exists, but sustained fresh evidence has not yet established Strong.
3. **Strong** — current mastery is at least 80% with no unresolved required intervention.

Safety/compliance requirements always override an otherwise passing percentage until their required recovery condition is satisfied.

## Instructor-facing rule

The primary instructor surface should show:
- **one score** — the existing certified mastery/grade result;
- **one status** — Strong, Improving, or Needs Attention;
- **one recommended action** — the action associated with that status.

Detailed evidence remains available underneath for drill-down; it must not compete with the primary status.

## Non-negotiable compatibility constraints

- Do not change the certified 20/10/40/15/15 calculation.
- Do not change ordinary 80% recovery requirements.
- Do not weaken 100% urgent bodily-safety recovery where required.
- Preserve Chapter 19's certified safety exception and Chapter 21's compliance semantics.
- Preserve original misses and diagnostic history after recovery.
- Preserve same-school authorization and student privacy.
- Do not reopen or modify Chapters 1–21 unless a regression or explicitly approved new requirement requires it.
- Status is a presentation/decision layer over certified evidence, not a second grading system.
