## Refinement proposals — not source changes

### HS-051 (ringed hair)
- Current publisher-framed item treats ringed hair as a variation of canities and gray/pigmented banding. The visually reviewed scanned textbook p256 explicitly uses that description.
- Proposed source-neutral question: “A hair shaft appears to have alternating lighter and darker bands along its length. Which condition matches this appearance?”
- Correct answer: “Ringed hair (pili annulati).”
- Proposed rationale: “Ringed hair shows a characteristic banded appearance. Appearance alone does not establish that pigmentation alternates.”
- **Open:** reconcile textbook taxonomy with independent dermatologic mechanism; review whether phrase or distractor structure is independently authored. Do not label the source 'wrong' solely on a terminology mismatch.

### HP-012 (chemical-service safety)
- Assembled hardening replaces options and **retains** the phrase “coarse texture may require stronger processing”; editing only the original question object would not fully correct the assembled experience.
- Proposed prompt: “A client has coarse strands, high porosity, and poor elasticity. What is the safest conclusion before considering a chemical service?”
- Proposed keyed response: “Assess all findings together; high porosity and weak elasticity warrant caution and appropriate strand/compatibility checks under the product instructions before deciding if service is appropriate.”
- Proposed rationale: “Strand diameter does not independently dictate product strength. Hair condition and manufacturer's directions must control chemical-service suitability.”
- **Open:** author distinct, plausible distractors and validate service-specific technical accuracy.

### HP-020 (salt bonds)
- Key A is supported as a pH-sensitive ionic interaction in barbering instruction.
- Proposed rationale: “Salt bonds are electrostatic side-bond interactions affected by the hair's pH environment; changes in pH can disrupt those interactions.”
- Remove or qualify the unsupported quantitative claim about 'one-third' of strength unless a separate verified citation establishes exactly what is measured.
- **Open:** assess overlapping HP-038 and whether it adds a different cognitive skill.

### HP-050 (new quality flag)
- Existing key A remains the best of four supplied choices, but the stem tests conceptual importance without requiring an applied safety judgment; the distractors are implausible.
- Potential independent alternative: ask whether brittle, highly porous hair should be treated without a test; key should favor caution, following manufacturer guidance and declining unsafe treatment.
- **Open:** validate proposed service scenario and copyrighted expression separately.
