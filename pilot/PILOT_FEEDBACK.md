|---|---|

## Guidelines

1. **Log all feedback** — Even if not actionable
2. **Categorize by role** — Student, Instructor, Admin
3. **Classify type** — Bug, Feature, Usability, General
4. **Note action taken** — Even if "No action"
5. **Review weekly** — Identify patterns and priorities
