# Welcome to ASCYN PRO — Pilot Program

**Welcome, Pilot Participants!**

You are among the first users to experience ASCYN PRO, an AI-powered professional licensing education platform designed to help students pass their board exams with confidence.

---

## About This Pilot

ASCYN PRO is currently in **pilot phase** — a limited release to selected users who will help us refine the platform before broader launch. Your participation is invaluable.

### Pilot Objectives

| Objective | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Validate Core Workflows** | Ensure students can study, take quizzes, and track progress effectively |
| **Test Instructor Tools** | Verify instructors can monitor students, manage grades, and track attendance |
| **Identify Issues** | Find bugs, usability problems, and areas for improvement |
| **Gather Feedback** | Learn what works well and what needs refinement |
| **Measure Outcomes** | Track engagement, completion rates, and user satisfaction |

## Support & Reporting

### Getting Help

| Issue Type | Contact | Response Time |
|------------|---------|---------------|
| **Critical** (system down, cannot login) | Gabriel Arcaina | Immediate |
| **High** (major feature broken) | Technical Lead | < 24 hours |
| **Medium** (feature partially works) | Technical Lead | < 1 week |
| **Low** (minor issue, cosmetic) | Log for post-pilot | Backlog |

### How to Report Issues

1. **Document the issue:**
   - What were you trying to do?
   - What happened instead?
   - What browser/device are you using?
   - Screenshots if helpful

2. **Report via:**
   - Email to Gabriel Arcaina
   - Direct message to Technical Lead
   - In-person if applicable

3. **Include:**
   - Your role (student/instructor)
   - Date and time of issue
   - Steps to reproduce

### Feedback Process

We want to hear from you! Please share:

- **What works well** — Features you find useful
- **What's confusing** — Anything unclear or hard to use
- **What's missing** — Features you wish existed
- **Suggestions** — Ideas for improvement

**Feedback Methods:**
- Informal conversation
- Email summary
- Weekly check-in meetings
- End-of-pilot survey
